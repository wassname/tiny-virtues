# Islamic: The qualities of men and women promised forgiveness (Qur'an 33:35)

## Muslim men and women

The first pair in 33:35 (submission), per Ali note 3718; equal for both sexes per note 3719.

> For Muslim men and women,- for believing men and women… for them has Allah prepared forgiveness and great reward.

Qur’an 33:35, trans. Abdullah Yusuf Ali

> 3718 Islam, or submitting our will to God's Will, includes all the virtues, as particularly specified in this verse. See n 3720

Abdullah Yusuf Ali, Holy Quran commentary (1934), note 3718

> 3719. …these virtues are as necessary to women as to men…

Abdullah Yusuf Ali, Holy Quran commentary (1934), note 3719

'Includes all the virtues' overlaps the other nine. A real case need not use religious words.

## believing men and women

The second pair in 33:35, which is item (1) of Ali note 3720.

> For Muslim men and women,- for believing men and women…

Qur’an 33:35, trans. Abdullah Yusuf Ali

Abdullah Yusuf Ali, Holy Quran commentary (1934), note 3720

Note 3720 gives no creedal list. Overlaps submission and remembrance.

## men and women who are patient and constant

Qur'an 33:35 and 2:177 (electronic Yusuf Ali text) with Yusuf Ali's 1934 notes 3719–3720.

> ... for men and women who are patient and constant ... for them has Allah prepared forgiveness and great reward.

Qur'an 33:35, Yusuf Ali electronic Y text

> ... the chief stress is laid on the fact that these virtues are as necessary to women as to men. ... **(4) patience and constancy, in suffering and in right endeavour**

Yusuf Ali notes 3719–3720, pp. 1116–1117

> ... to fulfil the contracts which ye have made; and to be firm and patient, in pain (or suffering) and adversity, and throughout all periods of panic.

Qur'an 2:177, patience in suffering, adversity and panic

suffering and right endeavour are alternative settings; constancy in right endeavour needs no suffering.
